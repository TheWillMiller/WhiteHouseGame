# Furniture proportions and seated view

The previous human-scale correction exposed oversized legacy furniture. The desktop was 4.65 m wide and 1.325 m high, chair cushions commonly stood .72–1.045 m above the old base, and bedroom/kitchen objects also used oversized dimensions. The room shell and human scale are preserved; individual furniture assemblies now have independent physical sizes, with attached details and corresponding collision footprints transformed together.

The Resolute desktop now uses **1.8288 × 1.2192 × .8255 m** (72 × 48 × 32½ inches), above the room's .11 m carpet baseline. This comes from the actual White House desk record on PDF page 7 of the [Ford Library December 1974 document](https://www.fordlibrarymuseum.gov/sites/default/files/pdf_documents/library/document/0047/phw19741227-02.pdf), rather than a replica's dimensions. Other furniture uses approximate human dimensions, not claimed measurements of the exact current White House pieces. The character remains 1.9 m tall.

Corrected furniture includes the Oval Office seating/desk/coffee and side tables; State Room chairs, settees, round and dining tables; generic office desks and seating; meeting tables; press audience seating; beds/nightstands; kitchen counters, island and refrigerator; billiard table; piano; and smaller salon/library tables. Tall architectural built-ins, room shells and circulation widths retain their existing layouts. These are proportion corrections to procedural assets, not claims of finished museum-quality replacement models.

Seating sets a front three-quarter view at a consistent elevation rather than inheriting the approach angle. The camera targets the upper body above the desktop, retains manual look, and faces the seat direction in first person. The president's chair is moved toward the resized desk. Seat hip height is .64 m and the root moves slightly forward to clear the chair back; the actual shipped skin has soles at approximately .104 m in this pose, matching the rug baseline.

Seated camera collisions include furniture in the Oval Office. Only the occupied seat's proxy is excluded; resized small furniture has explicit heights to avoid being interpreted as full-height walls. The cart seating contract is unchanged.

Verification uses the actual generated/batched scene and shipped animation data, without browser screenshots: all 66 destinations remain reachable; furniture bounds are finite; Resolute footprint matches the measurement; 90 seated entries span 15 seats, three arrival headings and portrait/landscape viewports; head sightlines are tested against actual rendered furniture triangles, body framing is checked, and first-person/stand-up transitions remain valid. Floor, State Room, player-skin and type checks also apply. This is not physical-device visual QA.

Replacement asset research: free CC Attribution candidates include [Emm's Louis XVI armchair scan](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/day-181-louis-xvi-armchair-1scanaday-fb90e13b599b44b799d1432b1679f9c2) and [AFig_'s Medallion Classic Chair](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/medallion-classic-chair-b4067b3b960e492b84786ae575d8bfd7). Download availability, visual quality and texture/geometry budgets must be inspected before integration. No new commercial model was purchased in this patch.
