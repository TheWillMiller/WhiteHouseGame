WHITE HOUSE RESIDENCE

This work is based on "The White House"
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/the-white-house-dbdb320ba4c6427ca4f2b2c2438034f9
by Void (https://sketchfab.com/Void.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: main residence extracted from the complex; presentation tilt removed;
model scaled and oriented for the game; geometry simplified and compressed;
textures optimized; ground-level collision and entrance markers added separately.
The original model includes both wings. Those portions are not in the current
game asset. The game uses its own interiors and construction scene.
