SOUTH LAWN HELIPAD SEAL
Vector adaptation of the public-domain Seal of the President of the United States.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seal_of_the_President_of_the_United_States.svg
Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/36/Seal_of_the_President_of_the_United_States.svg
Source lists a U.S. federal government work (Executive Office of the President).
Adapted September 21, 2026: monochrome granite palette, circular legend rebuilt
as vector outlines, 1024-pixel intrinsic resolution for bounded GPU memory.
Used as an architectural detail in an unofficial fictional White House game.
